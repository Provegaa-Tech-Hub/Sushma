🚀 How to Use

  1. Open index.html in your browser
  2. Hover over any person card to reveal action buttons
  3. Click the "🎨 Style" button to open customization menu
  4. Choose from 4 shapes, customize colors, or add links
  5. Changes apply instantly with smooth animations
  6. Use "Reset to Default" to remove all customizations
