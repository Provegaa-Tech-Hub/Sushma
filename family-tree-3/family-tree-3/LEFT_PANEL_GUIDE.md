# Left Panel Guide - Family Tree Application

## Overview
The left sidebar provides quick access to shapes, photo assets, and decorative elements for your family tree.

---

## 📐 SHAPES TAB

### Card Shapes
Quick access to create or change person card shapes:

#### Available Shapes:
1. **Rectangle** - Sharp, formal corners
2. **Rounded** - Soft rounded corners (default)
3. **Circle** - Circular person cards
4. **Hexagon** - Modern hexagonal design

#### How to Use:
- **Click any shape** to get instructions on adding new family members
- **To change shape of existing person:**
  1. Click on the person card
  2. In Properties panel (right side), select desired shape
  3. Changes apply instantly

### Photo Shapes
Control how photos appear within person cards:

#### Available Photo Shapes:
1. **Circle** - Traditional circular photo frame
2. **Square** - Square photo frame
3. **Rounded** - Square with rounded corners

#### How to Use:
- Select a person card with a photo
- In Properties panel, scroll to "PHOTO SHAPE" section
- Click desired shape to apply

## 🖼️ ASSETS TAB (Photo Upload)

### Upload Photos

#### Method 1: Sidebar Upload
1. Click on **Assets** tab in left sidebar
2. Click the **"Click to upload photos"** area
3. Select an image (JPG or PNG, max 5MB)
4. Photo is added to "Recent Uploads" gallery
5. If a person is selected, you'll be asked if you want to add it to them

#### Method 2: Direct Upload via Properties Panel
1. Click on any person card
2. In Properties panel (right side), click **"Upload Photo"**
3. Select image file
4. Photo is automatically added to the person

#### Method 3: From Recent Uploads Gallery
1. Upload photos via Assets tab
2. They appear in "Recent Uploads" gallery (shows last 6 photos)
3. Click any thumbnail to add it to currently selected person

### Managing Uploaded Photos
- **Recent uploads** show the last 6 photos
- Click any thumbnail to reuse it on another person
- Photos are stored in browser memory during the session

## 🎯 Complete Workflow Examples

### Example 1: Adding a Person with Photo

1. **Add the person:**
   - Click "+ Child" on an existing family member
   - Fill in Name and Gender (required)
   - Click "Add Child"

2. **Add their photo:**
   - Click on the newly added person card
   - In Properties panel, click "Upload Photo"
   - Select image file
   - Photo is automatically added

3. **Customize photo shape:**
   - In Properties panel, scroll to "PHOTO SHAPE"
   - Choose Circle, Square, or Rounded
   - Change applies instantly

### Example 2: Batch Photo Upload

1. **Upload multiple photos:**
   - Go to **Assets** tab
   - Click upload area
   - Select first photo
   - Repeat for more photos
   - All appear in "Recent Uploads"

2. **Apply to family members:**
   - Click on first person card
   - Click thumbnail from Recent Uploads
   - Photo is added
   - Repeat for other family members

### Example 3: Changing Card Shape

1. **Select person:**
   - Click any person card
   - Properties panel opens on right

2. **Change shape:**
   - Scroll to "SHAPE" section
   - Click Rectangle, Rounded, Circle, or Hexagon
   - Shape changes immediately with animation

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Photo Quality
- **Recommended:** Square photos (1:1 ratio) work best
- **Resolution:** 500x500px minimum for good quality
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Size:** Under 5MB per image

### Photo Shapes by Use Case
- **Circle:** Best for portrait photos, traditional look
- **Square:** Modern, works well for group photos
- **Rounded:** Versatile, softer than square

### Card Shapes by Generation
- **Suggestion:** Use consistent shapes per generation
  - Gen 1 (oldest): Rectangle
  - Gen 2-4: Rounded
  - Gen 5+ (youngest): Circle
- This creates visual hierarchy in your tree

### Organizing Photos
- Upload all photos at once via Assets tab
- Use Recent Uploads as a quick gallery
- Photos persist during your session
- Before closing, export your tree to save layouts

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Photo Won't Upload
- **Check file size:** Must be under 5MB
- **Check format:** Only JPG, PNG supported
- **Try resizing:** Use image editor to reduce size

### Can't See Recent Uploads
- Photos only show in Assets tab
- Maximum 6 thumbnails displayed
- Most recent photos appear first

### Photo Looks Distorted
- Original photo may not be square
- Try different photo shape (Square vs Circle)
- Use image editor to crop to 1:1 ratio first

### Shape Doesn't Change
- Ensure you clicked the shape in Properties panel (right side)
- Left panel shapes are informational/defaults
- Refresh page if shapes don't apply
